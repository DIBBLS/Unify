What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites and applications remember user preferences and activity. Unify Learn uses cookies and similar technologies (such as localStorage) to improve user experience, analyze platform usage, and maintain platform functionality.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for login authentication, account security, and core platform functionality. These cannot be disabled without breaking the platform.

  • Login authentication state
  • Account security tokens
  • Core platform functionality

Analytics Cookies

Used to understand how users interact with the platform, improve features and performance, and track errors and crashes.

Preference Cookies

Used to save user settings such as theme preference (light/dark mode), remember layout preferences, and improve overall user experience.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into Unify Learn — such as Google Firebase for authentication and analytics — may place cookies or use similar storage on your device for analytics or functionality purposes. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they use such data.

Managing Cookies

Users may disable cookies through their browser settings. Please note that some features of Unify Learn — including login and saved preferences — may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored by websites
  • Delete cookies individually or in bulk
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Set preferences for how cookies are handled
