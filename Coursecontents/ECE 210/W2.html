Week 2 of 11 Series, Parallel, Air Gaps & The B-H Curve

This is where you actually solve magnetic circuit problems. The B-H curve links H to B for each material and is used in every multi-material problem. The air gap always dominates — a 2mm gap typically needs more MMF than 100mm of steel.

Topic 2.1
Series Circuits, Air Gaps & Ampere's Circuital Law

In a series magnetic circuit, the same flux φ passes through all sections. Ampere's Circuital Law: NI = Σ(HᵢLᵢ) — sum H×L for every section around the loop. That total = MMF provided by the coil.

For an air gap: no B-H curve. Use H_gap = B/μ₀ = 7.96×10⁵ × B directly. Because air has μᵣ=1, even a tiny gap dominates the circuit.

5-step procedure for series magnetic circuits
1. Find B = φ/A (same for all sections in series)
2. From B-H curve: read H for each iron/steel section
3. For air gap: H_gap = B/μ₀ = 7.96×10⁵ × B
4. Ampere's Law: NI = H_core×L_core + H_gap×L_gap + ...
5. Solve for I = NI/N
Worked Example — Cast Steel Core with Air Gap

φ=0.75×10⁻⁴ Wb, A=1.5×10⁻⁴ m², N=200, L_core=100mm, L_gap=2mm. H_core at B=0.5T: 290 A/m.

B = φ/A = (0.75×10⁻⁴)/(1.5×10⁻⁴) = 0.5 T

H_gap = B/μ₀ = 0.5/(4π×10⁻⁷) = 3.98×10⁵ A/m

NI = 290×0.1 + 3.98×10⁵×0.002 = 29 + 796 = 825 A·t

I = 825/200 = 4.125 A

I = 4.125 A  ·  Note: 2mm gap (796 A·t) >> 100mm steel (29 A·t)
Common mistake
Trying to use a B-H curve for air — there isn't one. For air ALWAYS use H_gap = B/μ₀ = 7.96×10⁵ × B. Memorise this.
Topic 2.2
B-H Curve & Hysteresis

The B-H curve links field intensity H (applied) to flux density B (result) for a material. Starts linear, curves over at saturation. Materials: Sheet steel (highest μ), Cast steel (medium), Cast iron (lowest, cheapest).

The hysteresis loop shows energy lost as heat when magnetising/demagnetising cyclically. Key points: Bₘ (saturation), Bᵣ (remanence — flux when H=0), Hc (coercive force — H needed to make B=0). Loop area = energy loss per cycle per unit volume.

The distinction that loses exam marks every year
Eddy current loss: caused by induced circulating currents in solid iron rotating in magnetic field. Remedy: laminate the core (thin insulated layers break up current paths).

Hysteresis loss: caused by magnetic domain realignment each cycle. Remedy: use silicon steel (narrow hysteresis loop — material choice, not structural).

Laminations → eddy current loss. Silicon steel → hysteresis loss. Never mix these up.
