Topic 8.1
OC Test, SC Test & Maximum Efficiency

Open Circuit Test — LV side excited at rated voltage, HV side open. No secondary current → zero copper loss. Wattmeter reads iron (core) loss only. Gives P_iron and magnetising branch (Ro, Xo).

Short Circuit Test — HV side excited at reduced voltage (3–8% rated), LV side shorted. Tiny flux → negligible iron loss. Wattmeter reads full-load copper loss. Gives P_cu, Req, Zeq, Xeq.

Max efficiency when W_i = W_cu: Iron loss is constant (independent of load). Copper loss = x²×W_cu(FL) where x = fraction of full load. Setting equal: x = √(W_i/W_cu(FL)).

Why kVA not kW? Iron loss depends only on voltage (constant at rated voltage). Copper loss depends only on current. Neither depends on power factor. Total loss = f(V,I) = f(kVA) — independent of cosφ.

Transformer efficiency and tests
η = V₂I₂cosφ / (V₂I₂cosφ + W_i + I²Req)
Max η condition: W_i = I²Req = x²×W_cu(FL)
x_maxη = √(W_i / W_cu(FL))
Req = P_sc/I_sc²    Zeq = V_sc/I_sc    Xeq = √(Zeq²−Req²)
Worked Example

W_i=200W, W_cu(FL)=400W. Find load fraction at maximum efficiency.

W_i = x²×W_cu(FL) → 200 = x²×400 → x = √0.5 = 0.707

Maximum efficiency at 70.7% full load
Common mistake
OC test → LV side (LV excited, HV open). SC test → HV side (HV excited, LV shorted). Reversing these loses all marks. Also: maximum efficiency ≠ full load — it depends on W_i/W_cu ratio.
