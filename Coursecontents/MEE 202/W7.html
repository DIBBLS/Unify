Topic 7.1Space Lattice, Unit Cell & Basis

A space lattice is a regular periodic arrangement of points in space over long distances. It is strictly a geometric entity and ordinarily has nothing to do with atoms. In a space lattice the surrounding of each point is identical — this means that for all lattice points the nearest neighbours, the second nearest neighbours, and so on ad infinitum must be identical with respect to the direction and locations of the points in each neighbourhood group.

The unit cell is the smallest repeated unit in a structure. Think of it like the tile pattern on a floor — one tile is the unit cell, the whole floor is the lattice.

A crystal lattice (crystal structure) is a group of atoms identical in species and orientation attached identically to each lattice point. The identical group of atoms attached identically to each lattice point is called the Basis.

The Fundamental Relationship
Lattice + Basis = Crystal Structure
A primitive cell is a cell that contains only one lattice point.
P = Primitive cell · F = Face centred cell · I = Body centred cell · C = Base centred cell · R = Rhombohedral
💡 Think of it this way
The space lattice is like a grid of dots on graph paper — pure geometry, no atoms. The basis is the atom (or group of atoms) you glue to every dot. The result — dots + atoms — is the crystal structure. Diamond has an FCC lattice with a 2-atom basis (one C at the corner, one displaced ¼ along the body diagonal).

Topic 7.3Atoms Per Unit Cell & Atomic Packing Factor

In counting atoms in a unit cell, atoms are shared between cells. Corner atoms are shared by 8 cells (count ⅛ each), face atoms by 2 cells (count ½ each), edge atoms by 4 cells (count ¼ each), body-centre atoms belong entirely to one cell (count 1 each).

Atoms Per Unit Cell
N = Ninterior + Nface/2 + Ncorner/8
Simple Cubic: N = 0 + 0 + 8/8 = 1
BCC: N = 1 + 0 + 8/8 = 2
FCC: N = 0 + 6/2 + 8/8 = 4
Atomic Packing Factor (APF)
APF = (Number of atoms × Volume of one atom) / Volume of unit cell
FCC derivation: Face diagonal = 4R = a√2 → a = 2√2 R
Volume of unit cell = a³ = (2√2R)³ = 16√2 R³
APF = 4 × (4/3)πR³ / 16√2 R³ = π/(3√2) ≈ 0.74

BCC derivation: Body diagonal = 4R = a√3 → a = 4R/√3
APF = 2 × (4/3)πR³ / (4R/√3)³ = π√3/8 ≈ 0.68

Simple Cubic: a = 2R, APF = π/6 ≈ 0.52
Density of Crystalline Material
ρ = (n × w) / (NA × Vcell)
n = number of atoms per unit cell · w = atomic weight · NA = Avogadro's number (6.02 × 10²³) · Vcell = volume of unit cell
⚠ Common Mistake
Students mix up the face diagonal and body diagonal. For FCC: the face diagonal = 4R (atoms touch along the face). For BCC: the body diagonal = 4R (atoms touch through the body). Never use the edge length as 4R — the edge length is a, derived from the relevant diagonal.
★ Why This Matters
APF calculation has appeared in every exam. For FCC, APF = 0.74 — this is also the packing fraction for HCP (both are close-packed structures). BCC = 0.68. Simple cubic = 0.52. The 2021/22 paper also asks to derive the density–packing density relationship: ρ = APF × (mass of atoms)/(volume of unit cell). State your assumption: atoms are hard spheres and the material is perfectly crystalline.
