Wind Energy

It uses high wind velocity to generate power. California (USA) generates. 500 MW from 900 wind mills. The energy can be used for pumping the water /power generation. Approximately 0.7 million wind pumps are needed to suffix the demand of the world. A minimum wind velocity of 3m/s required to generate power, coastal hilly and valley areas are suitable for this purpose. Potential in India is estimated between 20 × 10⁴ to 2.5 × 10⁵ MW. Coastal areas of Gujarat, Maharashtra, Andhara Pardesh, Tamil Naidu are considered as favourable locations.

Topic 2.2.3
Biomass Energy

Its potential lies with agricultural residue. The estimated quantity of agricultural residue is 520 mT. ton out of which food grains contributes nearly 120 mT. The residues can be burnt directly for the production of steam. Or they may be gasified and gas so generated is used in an I.C. Engine for pumping or power generation. In case of forest wood the requirement of wood is between 250 to 360 mT and this quantity is not readily available. Bio gas can also be produced by anaerobic digestion of animal dung, excreta and sewage. Biomass may be a useful fuel for localized power generation in rural India.

Topic 2.2.5
Ocean Energy

Energy from ocean can be utilized either in form of wave, tide and/or ocean, thermal energy, about 13 kW power can be generated permeter of wave/tide height. A plant to generate 4.45 × 10⁵ kWH/YR is operating in Kerala. It generates 75 kW in calm months (from October to May) and 120-150 kW from June - September. It utilizes the temperature difference between warm surface sea water at around 28°C and the cold deep sea water at 4 to 5°C at a depth of 1000 to 1200 m in tropical areas. The golf of Kutch, Cambay gulf and Sunderban of Gujarat are potential sites. The total potential from sea is estimated as 5 × 10⁴ MW from ocean thermal energy conservation. Out of this 4 × 10⁴ MW can be obtained from wave energy and remains from tidal energy.

Topic 2.2.7
Magneto Hydro Dynamic (MHD) Energy

80% of the total electricity produced in the word is hydral while remaining 20% is produced from other sources such as thermal, nuclear, solar, geothermal and MHD sources of production. MHD power generation is a new system of power generation and claimed to be most efficient and low polluting sources. At the moment this technique is used in the advanced countries. In India this technique is under construction at Trichi (T.N.) under joint efforts of BARC, A.C.C. and Russian Government.

It is concerned with the flow of conducting fluid in the presence of magnetic and electrical fields. Fluid used may be either gas at elevated temperature or liquid metal viz. Sodium/potassium. An MHD generator is a device for converting heat energy of a fuel directly into electrical energy without a conventional electric generator. Here an MHD converter system is a heat engine; where heat taken up at a higher temperature is partly converted into useful electrical work and the remainder is rejected at lower temperature. The thermal efficiency of an MHD converter is increased by supplying the heat at the higher possible temperature and rejected at the lowest possible temperature.

It is the most promising of all the direct conversion techniques for large scale production of electric power. MHD is really of interest only for the central power generation, its potentialities for a propulsion units are remote.
