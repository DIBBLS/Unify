Week 12Systems of First-Order ODEs via Eigenvalues & Vector Spaces

The course ends with two applications. First: solving coupled first-order differential equations using eigenvalues — the system is written in matrix form, eigenvalues and eigenvectors are found, and the general solution is a linear combination of exponential terms. Second: a brief introduction to vector spaces, linear algebra of matrices, and how it all connects to the rest of the course.
