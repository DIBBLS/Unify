Week 5 of 12 Flywheel — Fluctuation of Energy & Speed

This week is where the numbers come in. You'll calculate exactly how much energy the flywheel stores, how much the speed fluctuates, and how to size a flywheel for a given engine. The coefficient of fluctuation of energy and coefficient of fluctuation of speed are the two key numbers.

  2. Calculate coefficient of fluctuation of speed C_s given N₁, N₂ or ω₁, ω₂.
  3. Find the energy stored in a flywheel given mass and radius of gyration. Common exam Q
Topic 5.1
Maximum Fluctuation of Energy (ΔE)

The turning moment diagram shows areas above and below the mean torque line. Areas above mean the engine is producing more than needed (storing energy in flywheel). Areas below mean the flywheel is releasing energy. The difference between the maximum and minimum energy levels during a cycle is called the maximum fluctuation of energy, ΔE.

Maximum fluctuation of energy
ΔE = E_max − E_min
ΔE = ½I(ω₁² − ω₂²) = Iω(ω₁ − ω₂) = Iω² · Cs
I = mass moment of inertia (kg·m²) · ω = mean angular speed (rad/s) · ω₁ = max speed · ω₂ = min speed · Cs = coefficient of fluctuation of speed
Coefficient of fluctuation of speed (Cs)
Cs = (N₁ − N₂) / [(N₁ + N₂)/2] [when given in rpm]
or Cs = 2(ω₁ − ω₂) / (ω₁ + ω₂) [when given in rad/s]
or Cs = 2(v₁ − v₂) / (v₁ + v₂) [when given as linear velocity]
N₁ = max speed (rpm) · N₂ = min speed (rpm) · Coefficient of Steadiness = 1/Cs
Coefficient of fluctuation of energy (C_E)
C_E = ΔE / (I · ω²) = ΔE / (T_max · θ)
ΔE = maximum fluctuation of energy · I·ω² = total work done per cycle
Energy stored in flywheel & moment of inertia
E = ½Iω²
I = mk²
ΔE = mk²ω² · Cs = 2·E·Cs
m = mass of flywheel (kg) · k = radius of gyration (m) · I = mk² (kg·m²)
Power of the engine
P = Tₘ · ω = Tₘ · 2πN/60
Tₘ = mean torque (N·m) · ω = angular speed (rad/s) · N = speed (rpm)
Worked Example — TMD for petrol engine (from notes)

TMD scale: 1mm = 5 N·m, crank handle 1mm = 1°. TMD repeats every half revolution. Rotating part mass = 36 kg, radius of gyration = 150mm, N = 1800 rev/min. Find coefficient of fluctuation of speed.

Step 1: Convert scale: 1mm² = 5 N·m × (π/180) = 5π/180 J = π/36 J
Step 2: From diagram, track energy at each point A→G. E_max at B = Φ+295; E_min at E = Φ−690
Step 3: ΔE = 295 + 690 = 985 mm² = 985 × π/36 = 681.86 J (≈ 685 J)
Step 4: ω = 2π(1800)/60 = 188.5 rad/s; I = mk² = 36×(0.15)² = 0.81 kg·m²
Step 5: Cs = ΔE/(Iω²) = 681.86 / (0.81 × 188.5²) = 681.86 / 28782 = Cs ≈ 0.0237
Common mistake
When tracking energy on the TMD, students forget that energy at A = 0 (set as reference). Energy ACCUMULATES as you add areas. Areas above mean line are positive (+), areas below are negative (−). Get one sign wrong and your ΔE is totally wrong.
