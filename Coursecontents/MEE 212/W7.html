Week 7 of 12 Prowell Governor & Performance Parameters

This week covers the loaded (gravity) governor analysis in detail, then finishes with the 6 key performance parameters every governor is judged on. These parameters are definition-heavy — expect them in exams.

Topic 7.1
6 Performance Parameters of a Governor
1. Sensitiveness (from notes)
Sensitiveness = (N_max − N_min) / N_min
Definition: The ability of the governor to maintain constant speed of rotation. Can be obtained mathematically as the ratio (N_max − N_min)/N_min.
2. Stability (from notes)
A governor is stable when for each speed within the working range, there is only one radius of the governor balls at which the governor is in equilibrium.
3. Isochronism (from notes)
A governor is said to be isochronous when the equilibrium speed is constant for all radii of rotation within the working range.
4. Hunting (from notes)
Hunting is a condition in which the speed of the engine controlled by the governor fluctuates continuously above and below the mean speed.
5. Governor Effort (from notes)
The effort of a governor is the force it can exert at the sleeve on the mechanism which controls the supply of fuel to the engine.
6. Power (from notes)
Power is defined as the work done at the sleeve for a given percentage change of speed.
Worked Example — Portal Governor (from notes)

Arms l = 200mm, r₁ = 125mm, W = 40N, kW = 200N (sleeve), F = 20N (friction). Find speed range.

Step 1 — h₁: h₁ = √(l² − r₁²) = √(200² − 125²) = √(40000−15625) = √24375 = 156.12 mm
Step 2 — ω₁²: ω₁² = (g/h₁)(1 + kW/W) = (9.81/0.15612)(1 + 200/40) = 62.84 × 6 = 378.02 rad²/s²
Step 3 — N₁: ω₁ = √378.02 = 19.44 rad/s → N₁ = 60×19.44/2π = 185.4 rpm
Step 4 — r₂ = 150mm: h₂ = √(200²−150²) = √(40000−22500) = √17500 = 132.28 mm
Step 5 — ω₂²: ω₂² = (9.81/0.13228)(1 + 200/40) = 74.16 × 6 = 444.91 → ω₂ = 21.09 rad/s
Step 6 — N₂: N₂ = 60×21.09/2π = 201.4 rpm
Answer — Speed Range: N₂ − N₁ = 201.4 − 185.4 = 16 rpm
Common mistake
Confusing isochronism and stability. An isochronous governor has ONE equilibrium speed for ALL radii — this means it is infinitely sensitive, which actually makes it unstable (prone to hunting). Stable ≠ isochronous.
