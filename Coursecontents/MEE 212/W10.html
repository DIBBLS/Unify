Week 10 of 12 Clutches

A clutch connects and disconnects power between two shafts. This week covers the definition, components, advantages/disadvantages, and the most important topic — calculating maximum pressure, minimum pressure and average pressure in a plate clutch under two theories: uniform wear and uniform pressure.

Exam focus

  3. Calculate frictional torque using T = nμWR. Distinguish uniform wear from uniform pressure theory.
Topic 10.1
Clutch — Definition, Components & Pressure Analysis
Exam-ready definition (from notes)
A clutch is a mechanical device that connects and disconnects the rotating shaft enabling the interaction of power transmission between 2 rotating components and engages 2 gear-changing. They help distribute load evenly.
Components of a clutch (7)
1. Clutch disk · 2. Pressure plate · 3. Flywheel · 4. Release bearing · 5. Spring · 6. (additional components per system)
Advantages & Disadvantages (from notes)
Advantages: Smooth engagement · Solid connection · User friendly & easy to engage · Transmits partial power
Disadvantages: Friction materials overheat · Burnout
Plate clutch pressure analysis — Uniform Wear Theory
C_mi = P_max × r₂ (constant)
P_max = C_mi / r₂ = kl / [2π(r₁ − r₂) × r₂]
P_min = C_mi / r₁
P_ave = kl / [π(r₁² − r₂²)]
Mean radius R = (r₁ + r₂) / 2 [Uniform wear]
r₁ = outer radius (mm) · r₂ = inner radius (mm) · kl = total axial force (N) = F
Frictional Torque
T = nμWR
Mean radius R = 2/3 × [(r₁³ − r₂³)/(r₁² − r₂²)] [Uniform pressure]
Mean radius R = (r₁ + r₂)/2 [Uniform wear]
n = number of pairs of friction surfaces · μ = coefficient of friction · W = total axial force · R = mean radius
Worked Example — Plate clutch (from notes 18-06-2025)

F = 4kN, r₁ = 100mm (outer), r₂ = 50mm (inner). Assume uniform wear. Find P_max, P_min, P_ave.

Total force on friction surface: kl = 2π × P_max × r₂ × (r₁ − r₂)
4000 = 2π × P_max × 50 × (100 − 50) = 2π × P_max × 50 × 50 = 15,708 × P_max
P_max = 4000/15,708 = 0.2543 N/mm²
P_min = C_mi/r₁ = (P_max × r₂)/r₁ = (0.2543 × 50)/100 = 0.1273 N/mm²
P_ave = kl/[π(r₁²−r₂²)] = 4000/[π(10000−2500)] = 4000/23,562 = 0.1699 N/mm²
Common mistake
Using R = (r₁+r₂)/2 for uniform PRESSURE — that's wrong. R = (r₁+r₂)/2 is for uniform WEAR only. For uniform pressure, R = 2/3 × [(r₁³−r₂³)/(r₁²−r₂²)]. Mixing these up costs big marks.
