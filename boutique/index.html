The Maison

A house built on a single belief.

Maison Lumière began as one woman's obsession with the well-made — the seam pressed by hand, the gold poured by an artisan who signs their work, the object that outlives its trend.

Today the boutique remains intentionally small. We carry less so that everything we carry can be the finest of its kind. To shop with us is to be styled, advised, and remembered.
