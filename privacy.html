Introduction

Welcome to Unify Learn ("we," "our," or "us"). Unify Learn is an educational platform designed to provide learning resources, courses, academic tools, and educational experiences for students and learners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username / profile information

Device & Technical Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address and browser type
  • App version

Usage Information

  • Pages visited and features interacted with
  • Learning progress and session duration

Analytics & Diagnostics

  • Crash logs, performance analytics, and error reports

2. Why We Collect Your Data

We collect data to:

  • Provide educational services
  • Improve app performance and personalize learning experiences
  • Maintain account security and fix bugs
  • Communicate updates and support information

3. Data Retention

We keep user data only as long as necessary to maintain accounts, provide services, meet legal obligations, and improve platform performance. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with analytics providers, cloud hosting services, and security and infrastructure providers. All third-party providers are expected to protect user information appropriately.

5. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Users may request:

  • Access to their data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of personal data
  • Restriction of processing
  • Export of their information

Requests can be made by contacting unifylearnn@gmail.com.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

Unify Learn does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent where required by law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the platform after updates means acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at unifylearnn@gmail.com.
